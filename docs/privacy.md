# PocketPort privacy policy

PocketPort transfers files you select from your Mac to your iPhone over your local Wi-Fi network. The Mac helper opens a temporary HTTP connection for the selected file. Use it on a trusted Wi-Fi network.

The iPhone app uses Camera access to scan the code displayed on your Mac and Local Network access to download the selected file. Camera images stay on your iPhone. PocketPort has no account, cloud service, advertising, or analytics. Your files are not sent to the developer or to GitHub.

Downloaded files and their names are stored on your iPhone so you can open them offline. Delete a file from the Files list or clear all downloads in Settings. If you share a copy using the iPhone share sheet, the destination you choose receives that copy.

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the [PocketPort repository](https://github.com/Pranav108/pocket-port). Please keep personal files out of public issues.
