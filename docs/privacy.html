Privacy Policy

How PocketPort transfers, stores, and shares your files.

On this page

Local file transfers Permissions Storage and deletion Sharing and services Contact and updates

Effective November 21, 2026

This policy covers the PocketPort app, the Mac helper, and this website. PocketPort transfers the file you select directly from your Mac to your Apple device. PocketPort has no user accounts, cloud file storage, advertising, or analytics. The app and Mac helper send no files or usage data to the developer.

Local file transfers

The Mac helper prepares the selected file and opens a temporary connection on your local network. The QR code contains the information the app needs to reach that connection, including a time-limited access token. Only share it with the intended recipient.

The connection uses HTTP on your local network. Use a trusted Wi-Fi network. The helper closes the share after the app acknowledges the transfer or the share expires.

Permissions

  • Camera reads the share QR code. The app processes the camera preview on your device and does not record, save, or upload camera footage.
  • Local Network lets the app download the file directly from your Mac and confirm the transfer.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in iOS or iPadOS Settings. Turning them off stops scanning or transfers, as applicable. You can still open previously downloaded files.

Storage and deletion

PocketPort keeps the downloaded file and details such as its name, project label, import date, size, and a checksum on your device. It uses them to verify the file, organize your library, and make it available offline. PocketPort also saves your delete-confirmation preference locally.

Downloaded copies stay in PocketPort until you delete them. Swipe a file in Files to delete it, or use Settings → Clear Downloaded Files to remove the entire library. Deleting a copy from PocketPort leaves the original on your Mac and any copies you shared in other locations.

Sharing and services

If you export a file through the system share sheet, the destination you select receives a copy. If you open an external link, the destination handles that visit under its own privacy practices.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitor IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This site contains no PocketPort tracking or analytics code. GitHub does not receive your local file transfers.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the PocketPort support repository. Requests and replies there are public. Leave out personal information, private files, and transfer codes.

We’ll update this page and its effective date if our practices change.
